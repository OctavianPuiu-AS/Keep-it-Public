Keep me

Lite version: non-commercial use, single-seat licence. Includes only the capture feature, limited to transforms, visibility, materials, lights, world, compositor and active camera.

Because of these limits, this version behaves slightly differently from the full add-on.

Unlike the full version, node setups (shaders, node groups, light, world and compositor nodes) are global. Changing a node tree affects all your captures. To use different setups, duplicate them by hand before capturing.

The upside of this limit is speed: the add-on does fewer operations.
