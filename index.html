What is Keep it?

Keep it is a scene-state and batch-render tool for Blender. Instead of manually toggling lights, swapping materials, and hiding objects every time you want a different version of your scene, you save each version once — then bring any of them back with a single click, or render every combination automatically.

You define your scene variations once (captures), your camera angles once (views), and your technical output requirements once (variants and profiles) — then Keep it handles every combination.

Keep it does three main things:

  1. Capture / Restore

    A saved state of your scene — lights, materials, visibility and more. Restore any of them at any time.

  2. View

    A saved camera angle, still or animated. Any view works with any capture.

  3. Render

    A job pairs captures with views and renders every combination.

Disclaimer

Keep it is a scene-state capture/restore and batch-render tool. To use it safely and set the right expectations, here's what it deliberately does not do.

  • Not a backup or version-control system
  • Does not capture mesh, curve, or geometry data itself
  • Does not capture animation (F-curves, actions) — except camera view animation
