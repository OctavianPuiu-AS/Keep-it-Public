    Things to try

    1. Capture a setup

      Change a colour, hide a cube, swing the light. Then press Capture. The whole look is saved as one card.

    2. Restore it

      Change everything again, then click the card. The scene goes straight back — nothing to remember, nothing to toggle by hand.

    3. Go back with Latest

      Restored the wrong one? Latest takes the scene back to how it was just before that click.

    4. Capture only part of it

      Turn off some chips under Capture. “Just the tower” only holds visibility and materials, so restoring it leaves your light and backdrop alone.

    5. Save a view

      Drag the picture to orbit, then press Save view. Views are camera angles. They live apart from captures, so any setup works with any angle.

    6. Render the sheet

      Render sheet draws every capture from every view. In Blender this is a render job: real renders, saved to disk, while you get on with something else.

    In Blender

    The playground keeps four things about three objects. The add-on works on your whole scene:

    • A capture holds much more than colours and a light.
    • Views are your real cameras, and can be animated.
    • Render jobs write real images, every capture from every view.
