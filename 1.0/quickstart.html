From zero to a batch render

This walkthrough gets you from zero to your first batch render in under five minutes.

Step by step

  1. Take your first capture

    Set up your scene the way you want it — lights, materials, visibility. In the main panel, make sure the categories you care about are toggled on, then press Keep it Alt C.

  2. Change the scene and capture again

    Adjust your lights or materials to a different look. Press Keep it Alt C again. You now have two captures.

  3. Switch between them

    Click between the two thumbnails in the gallery (or Alt ← / Alt →). Each click restores that version of the scene.

  4. Add views

    In the views panel, pick your camera. Keep it creates a view at its current position. Move the camera and press Add View Alt V. You now have two views.

  5. Create a render job

    In the render panel press Add Job. Select the captures and views. You now have 4 stills.

  6. Check the contact sheet

    Click the contact sheet button. A grid shows all 4 stills as thumbnails. Keep only what you want by unchecking, and adjust anything you want on an individual still by clicking the edit button.

  7. Render

    Press render job. Keep it restores each shot and renders it automatically, saving every file to disk. Files are saved to the job’s output path.
