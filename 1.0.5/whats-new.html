What’s new in 1.0.5

1.0.5

New

  • Arrange Captures. A new button beside the group filter lets you drag your captures into a new order. Each group keeps its own order. How it works
  • The Latest button is also the way back. Go to Latest, then press the same button again (now a back arrow) to return to the capture you were on.
  • Blender 5.3’s compositor effects stack. A capture now holds the whole stack — every effect, its settings and keyframes, the order and which one is selected — and a Compositor variant swaps the whole stack for its shots.
  • Passes warn when they won’t be saved. Only a multilayer EXR carries passes inside the render. With PNG, JPEG or a plain EXR and Write Separate Files off, the Passes section now tells you.

Better

  • Clicking a capture is fast again in animated scenes. Keep it now puts back only the animated values instead of refreshing the whole scene — on a heavy test scene, from 2.6 s back to 1.35 s per click.
  • Go to Latest keeps your latest work. What you did since your last click is saved before Latest is shown, the same as a click does.
  • One “KeepIt Hidden” per scene. With two scenes in a file, each scene’s Hide New objects stay in its own collection.
  • Add-ons enabled mid-session are captured. A property an add-on or render engine registers after Keep it started is now captured and restored, without restarting Blender.

Fixed

  • Latest keeps your work right after taking, editing or pushing into a capture, and after Browse Captures.
  • Unbaked simulations no longer restart when you click a capture or render a queue.
  • Two scenes in one file each keep their own gallery, camera list and captures.
  • Drivers survive a rebuild. Restoring a material’s node tree or a modifier stack no longer strips their drivers.
  • Objects hidden with the eye stay hidden when a restore brings their collection back.
  • Hair comes back with its viewport and render visibility, in its place in the modifier stack.
  • Material slots switch back to their mesh when the capture had them there.
  • Compositor links from passes the current engine doesn’t have (Cycles-only passes on EEVEE) are kept.
  • Contact-sheet cells compose their own capture, and Undo back into a cell then Go to Latest puts the camera back.
  • Colorways with two rows on the same slot put the original material back.
  • Brought-back objects find a collection that was renamed, and Hide New never takes an object out of the scene.
  • Keep me can be disabled, re-enabled and updated cleanly.
